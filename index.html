The premise

Gravity is the last impurity.

three parts
  1. 01

    chapter 01

    Every furnace on Earth shares one flaw

    Convection stirs the melt. The boule sags under its own weight. The lattice inherits both, and no amount of chilling, spinning or doping removes them — you cannot switch off the field.

  2. 02

    chapter 02

    In free fall the melt goes still

    Heat moves by conduction alone. The solid-liquid interface stays flat to within a few microns, and the seed draws a column that is straight in a way no terrestrial puller can match.

  3. 03

    chapter 03

    Nothing about the chemistry is new

    We use the same Czochralski pull that Poland described in 1916. The only variable we removed is the one nobody could reach. Vespera-1 has drawn boules continuously since March 2031.

The method

Four things stop happening at 412 km.

Convection at 412 km

0cm/s

With no buoyancy there is no stirring. Dopant lands where it is placed and stays there.

Interface deflection at 412 km

3µm

The growth front stays flat across the full 300 mm face instead of bowing under load.

Boule sag at 412 km

0.0mm

A two-metre column carries no weight, so it cannot creep while it is still soft.

None

Containerless floating-zone growth removes the last source of contamination entirely.

Trajectory

Sixteen years from a dropped capsule to a foundry.

  1. 2028

    Drop tower

    Four seconds of free fall in Bremen produced a lattice nobody could explain. The company existed by the end of that month.

  2. 2029

    Sounding rocket

    Six minutes of microgravity, one seed crystal, one usable centimetre. Enough to raise the first round.

  3. 2031

    First pull in orbit

    Vespera-1 draws a 100 mm silicon boule over eleven days. It comes down whole.

  4. 2034

    Continuous operation

    The furnace stops being an experiment. Three pulls run in parallel, uninterrupted, for the first full year.

  5. 2036in vessel

    Two metres

    The longest single crystal ever grown, in any gravity. Currently in the vessel.

  6. 2038planned

    Vespera-2

    A second station dedicated to containerless growth, with a return cadence of eleven days.
